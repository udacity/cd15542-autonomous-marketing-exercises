# Campaign Brief — Momentum Collection Launch

**Brand:** Solstice Active
**Requested by:** Marketing Lead
**Campaign type:** Google Ads Search — RSA

## Product

Momentum Collection: new legging and jogger line.

- **Leggings:** high-waist, four-way stretch, squat-proof opacity, compression
  fit through the hip and thigh, sweat-tested moisture-wicking fabric.
- **Joggers:** tapered fit, same fabric family as the leggings, side pockets
  sized for a phone, elastic cuff that doesn't ride up mid-run.
- Both pieces are designed for the studio-to-street crossover — built for a
  workout, worn after it without changing.

## Audience

Women 25–40 who train 3+ times a week (studio classes, running, lifting) and
also want the piece to work for errands/school-run immediately after. They
read fabric specs before buying and distrust vague "flattering"/"comfort"
claims with nothing under them.

## Offer / promo

Launch week: 20% off the Momentum Collection, first 10 days only. Free
returns within 30 days (standing policy, always safe to mention).

## Seed keywords

- High waist leggings squat proof
- Four way stretch leggings
- Workout leggings that don't roll down
- Women's joggers with pockets
- Studio to street activewear
- Compression leggings for lifting
- Sweat wicking leggings women

## Landing page

`solsticeactive.com/momentum`

## Compliance notes

- Do not claim "waterproof" or medical/therapeutic benefits (compression is
  a fit claim, not a medical one) — fit claims only.
- Sizing: line runs true to size XS–3XL; if a headline references sizing,
  it must not overpromise ("fits everyone") — keep it neutral or omit.
- Returns claim must say "30 days," not "unlimited" or "anytime."
