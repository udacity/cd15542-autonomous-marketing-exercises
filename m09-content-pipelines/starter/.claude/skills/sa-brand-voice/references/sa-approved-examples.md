# Solstice Active — approved examples

Real brand-approved lines, organized by channel. Calibrate against the one
closest to what you're writing. Don't copy the wording — match the register:
specific, direct, motivational because it's earned.

## Same fact, done right vs. wrong

**Wrong (generic hype, no product under it):**
> Crush your workout in our ultimate leggings. No excuses — dominate your day!

**Right (same product, specific and earned):**
> High-waist compression that holds through burpees, squats, and the school
> run after. Four-way stretch, sweat-tested.

The wrong version could be pasted onto any activewear brand's ad and lose
nothing. The right version is unmistakably about this specific product.

## RSA-style headline lines (≤30 characters)

- Squat-Proof. Sweat-Tested.
- Studio to Street, No Change
- High-Waist, Zero Roll-Down
- Four-Way Stretch Leggings
- Built to Move With You
- New Drop: Momentum Joggers

## RSA-style description lines (≤90 characters)

- Compression fit that holds through burpees and the school run after. Shop the drop.
- Sweat-tested fabric, high-waist band that doesn't roll. Train in it, wear it after.
- Four-way stretch built for squats, not just standing still. Free returns, 30 days.

## Social caption

> The waistband that doesn't roll down mid-set. That's the whole pitch for
> the Momentum leggings — squat-tested, not just marketed that way.

## Product-launch line

> Momentum Collection: leggings and joggers built for the studio-to-street
> stretch, tested through actual squats, not just a photo shoot.
