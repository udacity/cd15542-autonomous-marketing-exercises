# Tracked Competitors — Groundswell Competitor Watch

Scope: juice and wellness brands sold in US retail (grocery, natural, mass). DTC-only brands are out of scope unless they've moved onto shelves.

The list is tiered by closeness to a cold-pressed juice business. When ranking the top 10 news items, weight Tier 1 above Tier 2 above Tier 3. A Tier 1 product launch beats a Tier 3 funding round.

Search each brand by name plus its parent company, since a lot of the real news (pricing, distribution, reformulation) breaks at the parent level.

## Tier 1 — Direct: cold-pressed / premium juice

These are the closest competitors. A move here is almost always relevant.

- **Suja** (Suja Life) — organic cold-pressed juice and wellness shots
- **Pressed** (Pressed Juicery) — cold-pressed juice, shots, retail plus own stores
- **Evolution Fresh** — cold-pressed, HPP, wide grocery distribution
- **Naked Juice** (PepsiCo) — mass-market smoothies and juice
- **Bolthouse Farms** — refrigerated juice and smoothies, mass channel
- **BluePrint** (Hain Celestial) — cold-pressed, cleanse positioning
- **Little West** — California cold-pressed, no concentrates

## Tier 2 — Adjacent: wellness shots and functional juice

Overlaps directly with the shots side of a juice range. Track for launches, ingredient claims, and retail expansion.

- **So Good So You** — refrigerated wellness and probiotic shots
- **Vitaminis** — juice shots, gut and immune positioning, no added sugar
- **Sofresco** — cold-pressed functional shots and blends

## Tier 3 — Peripheral: broader functional beverages

Not juice, but competing for the same "healthy drink" dollar and shelf space. Track lightly — include only when the news is big (major launch, acquisition, national rollout).

- **Olipop** and **Poppi** (PepsiCo) — prebiotic sodas, the fastest-growing functional category and the clearest threat to juice shelf space
- **Cure Hydration** — plant-based electrolyte mixes, ~21,000 doors
- **Celsius** — functional energy with a wellness position
- **Recess** — calm/adaptogen drinks
- **Bai** (Keurig Dr Pepper) — antioxidant-positioned flavored water

## Scoping notes

- **Poppi** was acquired by PepsiCo in 2025, so Poppi news often surfaces through PepsiCo. Same logic applies to Naked (PepsiCo), BluePrint (Hain), and Bai (Keurig Dr Pepper) — check the parent.
- **Odwalla** is left off. Coca-Cola discontinued the US line in 2020, so it's not a live shelf competitor even though older market reports still list it.
- **Ceres** is imported (South African) shelf-stable juice. Include only if Groundswell competes in the ambient juice set rather than refrigerated cold-pressed.
- This is a starting set, not fixed. Add or drop brands as Groundswell's channel and region focus sharpens. The moment you know the specific retailers or regions you care about, tell the agent and it can prune the list to what shares your shelf.
