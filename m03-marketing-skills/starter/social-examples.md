# Groundswell Juice Co. — Social Media Examples

Past Instagram captions, for tone and structure calibration. 3–4 sentences, no hashtags, no emoji. Open on the fact or the moment, include one concrete specific even at this length, close on a plain confident line rather than a stacked-exclamation CTA.

## Product photo — Repair Red

Repair Red isn't just tart cherry for the sake of it. Tart cherry has real research behind its effect on post-workout soreness, mostly tied to its natural melatonin and anti-inflammatory compounds. We paired it with beet for circulation and ginger to settle your stomach after a hard effort. The cherries come from a family farm in Traverse City we've worked with for three seasons. That's the whole story, no exaggeration needed.

## Community photo — river cleanup

Saturday, our Riverside team traded the counter for gloves and trash bags. Volunteers pulled just under 900 pounds of debris from this stretch of river last year, and we're aiming higher this time. If you're local, the invite's open, no sign-up required. Just show up.

## Packaging photo — new cups

Our cups changed this spring. New plant-based lining, compostable through commercial facilities in 40 of our 55 cities so far. We're not going to call this "sustainable" and leave it there. We'll tell you exactly which cities are covered and which ones we're still working on.
