# GSJ Claims Tracker

Mirror of `gsj-claims-tracker.xlsx` (marketing/legal owned). This file is the source of truth for what Groundswell is approved and banned from claiming in copy. Do not approve or ban any claim from memory — use these rows.

## Approved claims

| ID | Claim | Proof Source | Limits / Notes |
|----|-------|-------------|----------------|
| A1 | Tart cherry supports post-exercise soreness recovery | General published research on tart cherry, melatonin and anti-inflammatory compounds | Do not upgrade to "clinically proven" — no Groundswell-run clinical trial exists |
| A2 | Beet supports blood flow / circulation | General nutritional research on dietary nitrates in beets | Keep as general mechanism, not a medical claim |
| A3 | Ginger settles the stomach | General nutritional research on ginger and digestion | Fine as stated, do not add "cures" or "eliminates" |
| A4 | Cherries sourced from a family farm in Traverse City, 3 seasons | Internal sourcing records, gsj-product-facts.pdf | Always name the farm and location when making a sourcing claim |
| A5 | Cups compostable in commercial facilities in 47 of 55 cities | Internal composting partner list, spring rollout, gsj-product-facts.pdf | Must state the specific number, never round up to "most" or "all" |
| A6 | Cup accepted in standard recycling where compost isn't available | Internal packaging spec, gsj-product-facts.pdf | Always pair with the compost coverage caveat |
| A7 | Riverside cleanup removed ~900 lbs of debris last year | Internal event records, gsj-product-facts.pdf | City-specific — do not apply this figure to other locations |

## Banned claims

| ID | Claim | Reason on file |
|----|-------|---------------|
| B1 | "Clinically proven" (any product, any benefit) | No claim in the product facts file is backed by a clinical trial — use "we checked" or "research shows" instead |
| B2 | "100% sustainable" / "eco-friendly" (unqualified) | Vague sustainability language is treated as dishonest per brand voice guide — always cite the specific number or program |
| B3 | "Detox" / "cleanse" (any product) | Banned brand-wide regardless of context, see gsj-voice-guide.pdf |
| B4 | "Guaranteed" results of any kind | No guarantee claim is supported by anything in product facts |
| B5 | Any claim that all 55 cities have composting today | Factually false — only 47 of 55 currently covered |
