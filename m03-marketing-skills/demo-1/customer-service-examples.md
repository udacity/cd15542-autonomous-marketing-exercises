# Groundswell Juice Co. — Customer Service Examples

Past customer service replies, for tone calibration. This isn't a separate format with its own structural rules — it's the same voice addressed to one person instead of an audience. What shifts: acknowledge the specific situation before anything else, offer a concrete remedy (a credit, an amount, a next step) rather than a vague apology, and when the news is bad (a complaint, a "no"), be honest that a tradeoff is a tradeoff rather than either over-apologizing or getting defensive.

## Complaint reply — order delay

**Situation:** Customer emailed saying their mobile order sat at the counter for 20 minutes and the smoothie was watery by the time they picked it up.

Hi Marcus,

Twenty minutes on a mobile order is too long, and a melted smoothie isn't what you paid for. That's on us.

We remade your order fresh and added $10 to your account for next time, no conditions attached.

I also flagged this specifically to our Denver team, because a 20-minute hold usually means something broke in how mobile orders get routed to the counter, not just that it was a busy afternoon. We're looking into what happened on our end so it doesn't repeat.

Thanks for telling us instead of just not coming back. That's genuinely more useful to us than a quiet loss of a customer.

Francine
Groundswell Customer Team

## Negative reply — didn't like the taste

**Subject:** Sorry Repair Red wasn't it

Hi James,

Thanks for the honest feedback. Repair Red runs tart by design, tart cherry and ginger both push in that direction, and we know it's not going to land for everyone. That's a real tradeoff, not a mistake on our end, but I get that knowing the reasoning doesn't make the cup in your hand taste better.

A few options if you want to give us another shot. Golden Hour is sweeter, mostly mango and pineapple with a small amount of turmeric, so it reads closer to what you're describing wanting. Or if you tell the person at the counter you found Repair Red too tart, they can add a splash of apple juice to round it out without changing what the blend does.

I've added a credit to your account for a replacement drink, no strings on which one you pick.

Nick, Groundswell Customer Team
