## Acceptable variations (live runs differ)
- **The writer got the HOA right in round 1.** It read the listing sheet as well as the brief. That's good behavior, and the auditor should pass the email.
- **The writer refused "guaranteed to appreciate."** Also fine. It happens most often when the learner gave the writer a no-value-predictions rule after Demo 2.
- **"Young professionals" shows up in a draft.** That's a blocker under brg-brand-voice rule 2, because it describes buyers by age. It counts as a correct third catch.
- **The auditor flags "move-in ready" or "price improved."** Both are approved for this listing on its sheet. Check that rule 2 in the learner's accuracy skill reads the "Approved language for this listing" section.

## Common issues
1. **The auditor handed back rewritten copy.** It has write access. Check its `tools:` line.
2. **The writer repeated the mistake in round 2.** It didn't receive the findings. Check step 3 of the workflow in CLAUDE.md.
3. **The findings don't cite rules.** Tighten the output format in the auditor's body.
4. **The auditor passed everything.** It's too lenient, or a skill didn't load. The brief produces errors on purpose.
5. **The writer and the auditor kept passing the same asset back and forth.** The workflow in CLAUDE.md is missing the one-round cap.
