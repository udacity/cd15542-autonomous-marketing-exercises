# Brand Voice — Bellwood Realty Group

These rules check the words on the page. They don't need the listing sheet, which is why the writer loads this skill too.

## Rules

1. **No stock pressure phrases.** Never use "won't last long," "motivated seller," "priced to sell," or "act fast." Severity: warning.
2. **Fair Housing language.** Never describe who a property or neighborhood suits based on family status, religion, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected class. This includes indirect phrasing: "perfect for a growing family," "great for empty nesters," "ideal starter home for young couples," "walkable for retirees." Describe the home, never the buyer. Severity: blocker.
3. **No unranked superlatives about schools or neighborhoods.** "Best schools in town," "top-rated district," "safest street." Severity: warning.
4. **No promises about the sale.** "Guaranteed to sell," "guaranteed offer," "will sell in days." Severity: blocker.
5. **No naming competing brokerages.** Refer to other agencies by category only ("other local brokerages," "the competition"), never by name. Severity: warning.

## Output when reviewing
For each finding: the exact line, this skill's name, the rule number, the severity, and a suggested fix.
