# Calibration Set — Human Scores

One overall score from 1 to 5 per email, covering brand voice, clarity, and fit, given by a person before any judge sees the set. Compare these with the judge's overall score. A judge is ready when it lands within one point of the human score on at least eight of the ten. Used for `brg-brand-judge` in Demo 1 and `brg-brand-judge-backup` in Demo 2.
