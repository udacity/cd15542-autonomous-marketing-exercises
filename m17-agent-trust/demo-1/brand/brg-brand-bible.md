# Bellwood Realty Group — Brand Bible

## Who they are
Bellwood Realty Group is a residential brokerage with about 40 agents across three suburban offices. Every agent posts their own listings on social and runs their own local ad spend, so the same brand-voice and compliance rules have to hold up across dozens of people who aren't marketers.

## Voice pillars
- Plain and local — no real-estate-agent stock phrases ("won't last long!", "motivated seller!").
- States facts, not pressure. Never implies urgency the data doesn't support.
- Warm, but never presumes who a home is for.

## Hard-banned language (Fair Housing Act)
This is the compliance backbone every skill and audit in this project checks against. Never describe who a property or neighborhood is suited for based on family status, religion, national origin, or similar. Never use an unranked superlative about schools or neighborhoods. Never promise an outcome or a timeline.

### Banned phrases
`brg-check-script.js` reads this list every time it runs. To ban a new phrase, add a line here. No code change is needed.
- perfect for families
- perfect for a growing family
- great for empty nesters
- ideal starter home for young couples
- ideal for retirees
- best schools in town
- guaranteed to sell
- guaranteed offer

## Approved language
Approval is per listing, never global. A phrase such as "move-in ready" is cleared only for a listing whose listing sheet includes it in its notes. The same phrase on any other listing is unverified.

## Required disclosure
Every listing email, ad, and listing page must include the equal-housing disclosure, word for word:
> Bellwood Realty Group is an Equal Housing Opportunity broker. All properties advertised are available without regard to race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, or national origin.
