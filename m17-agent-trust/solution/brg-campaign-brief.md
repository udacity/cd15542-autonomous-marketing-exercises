# Campaign Brief — Local Influencer Outreach for Open Houses

Bellwood's marketing team wants to promote its Maple Creek and Ashgrove-area open houses through local creators, not paid ads alone. For each campaign, `brg-influencer-scout` researches every candidate on the list and drafts an outreach email. This campaign's list has 20 candidates (`candidates/brg-candidate-list.md`). Nothing checks or scores what the scout returns yet. That's what this activity builds.

**The open house:** 142 Ashgrove Lane, in Maple Creek. Saturday, October 17, 2026, 1–3 pm. Hosted by listing agent Dana Ferris of Bellwood Realty Group. Every outreach email names this open house.

**Target audience:** local home buyers in Bellwood's metro service area. Bellwood never describes, targets, or scores an audience by family status, age, or any other protected class. Fit is about local relevance and content niche, not who the followers are.

**Follower range:** 5,000–150,000 followers. The check script drops any creator outside this range before the judge scores them. Inside the range, follower count is not a measure of fit.

**What counts as a good fit:**
- **Local relevance:** the candidate's content or audience is tied to Bellwood's service area, not generic or national.
- **Content niche:** neighborhood, lifestyle, home, or home-tour content. Luxury, travel, or fitness accounts are off-niche for this campaign, even with strong numbers.
- **Clean disclosure history:** any past sponsored post must show a paid-partnership disclosure. Bellwood's name will be on the post, and an undisclosed-ad history is a compliance risk it can't take on.

**What doesn't count as fit on its own:** follower count. A large following with the wrong niche or no local tie is a weaker fit than a smaller, well-matched local account. This campaign is measured on relevant reach, not raw reach.

**What a good outreach email does:** mentions something real and specific about the creator's own content, names the open house, and asks the creator to label any post as a paid partnership. A template with the name swapped in isn't good enough, however well it's written.

**Bellwood's voice:** plain and local, states facts, no pressure. A creator's own content doesn't need to match this voice, but anything Bellwood writes for them should.

**A person approves every send.** Nothing goes to a creator until someone at Bellwood has read it.
