# Solstice Active — approved voice examples

## Same fact, done right vs. wrong

**Wrong (generic hype, nothing underneath):**
> Unleash your potential in the Momentum Legging — engineered for those who refuse to settle! Feel unstoppable in every workout!

**Right (specific, confident, no hype punctuation):**
> The Momentum Legging uses a four-way stretch knit with a compression fit through the waistband, so it holds its shape through squats and stays opaque at full stretch. Built for training days that start before sunrise and don't end when the workout does.

The wrong version could be pasted onto any activewear brand's site with no edits. The right version names what the fabric actually does — that's the whole difference.

## Email (product launch)

> The Momentum Collection is up. Four-way stretch through the leggings, moisture-wicking through the sports bras, and a length on the joggers that actually works with sneakers, not just slides. Shop the full line before sizes shift.

Direct, specific, no urgency manufactured beyond a plain fact (sizes shift). No exclamation point needed.

## Social caption (Instagram)

> 5am or 5pm, the Momentum Legging doesn't care which one you show up for. Squat-proof at full stretch, four-way stretch through the knit. Link in bio.

Short, respects that training happens on the customer's schedule, not a prescribed one. One product-specific claim, no manufactured intensity.

## Customer service reply

> Thanks for flagging the sizing question — the Momentum Legging runs true to size for most people, but if you're between sizes, we'd size up for the compression fit through the waistband. Happy to help you swap if it's not right once it arrives.

Warm, plain, no performance of enthusiasm. States the actual fit behavior instead of a generic "we're sure you'll love it."
