# Jordan × Momentum Legging

**Customer:** Jordan M., runner training for her first full marathon
**Product:** Momentum Legging (compression fit)

## Problem
Jordan had been running for eight years, mostly 10Ks, before she signed up for her first full marathon. The jump in weekly mileage changed everything, including how her gear held up. Training five days a week, by week six or seven her leggings were rolling down at the waist on every run, which meant tugging at them instead of focusing on her pace.

## Approach
Jordan noticed that her training partner Divya's Momentum Legging waistband never moved, even on their long Sunday runs. Divya explained the compression fit built into the waistband, and Jordan decided to try a pair for herself.

## Outcome
The compression fit held from the first run: a rest-week five miler where Jordan realized she hadn't touched the waistband once. A 16-mile training run, her longest at that point, held the same way, staying in place two hours in even as her form loosened with fatigue. The fabric's four-way stretch also worked through her pre-run hip-opener routine, moving with her instead of restricting her lunges.

On marathon day, the leggings held for the full 26.2 miles, about four and a half hours, with no adjustment needed. For Jordan, that meant one less thing to manage on race day.
