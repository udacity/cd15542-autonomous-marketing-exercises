# Jordan — Solstice Active case study

## Problem

Jordan has been running for eight years, but training for a first full marathon was a different demand than the 10Ks they'd trained for before. Building up to five runs a week, Jordan hit a wall by week six or seven: their old leggings rolled down at the waist on every run. Past four miles, that meant tugging at the waistband instead of focusing on pace.

## Approach

Jordan noticed their training partner Divya running in the Momentum Legging on long Sunday runs, waistband staying put the whole way. Divya pointed to the compression fit built into the waistband, and Jordan decided to try a pair.

The first test was a rest-week easy five-miler: no waistband adjustments needed. A few weeks later, Jordan wore them for a 16-miler, their longest training run to that point, and got the same result, including in the later miles when their form had started to break down. Jordan also noticed the fabric's stretch held up through a pre-run routine of hip-opener stretches and lunges, moving with them instead of restricting the motion.

## Outcome

Jordan wore the Momentum Legging for the full marathon, about four and a half hours on course, without needing to adjust the waistband. The transcript doesn't include a finish time or pace, so no performance number is claimed here.
