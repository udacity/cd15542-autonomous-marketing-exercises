# Raw interview transcript — Priya K.

Product discussed: Pulse Sports Bra (high-impact)
Context: Customer interview conducted by Solstice Active marketing team for a case study. Speaker turns labeled below — do not treat unlabeled text as customer voice.

---

**INTERVIEWER:** Thanks for making time, Priya. Can you start by telling me a bit about what you do and what you were looking for in a sports bra?

**PRIYA:** Yeah, of course. So I teach spin and HIIT classes, six, sometimes seven days a week, so I'm basically in workout gear all day every day. And the big thing for me is just — I need something that holds up through like an hour of jumping and burpees and doesn't need constant adjusting, because I'm also demonstrating moves the whole time, I don't have a spare hand to fix my bra mid-class.

**INTERVIEWER:** Makes total sense. So what led you to try the Pulse?

**PRIYA:** Honestly one of my regulars in class wears Solstice stuff and mentioned the Pulse a few times, and I'd been using a CoreForm bra for like two years at that point, and it was fine, but the straps always dug in by the end of a long teaching day. So I figured I'd try something new.

**INTERVIEWER:** And how'd the first class go in it?

**PRIYA:** It was good! I taught a 45-minute HIIT class in it, lots of jump squats, mountain climbers, that kind of high-impact stuff, and I didn't feel like I needed to adjust it once, which for me is honestly the bar. I also noticed the band across the back doesn't dig in the way some others do, even by the end of a back-to-back double class day.

**INTERVIEWER:** Nice. Anything else stand out?

**PRIYA:** The material breathes really well, like I sweat a ton teaching six classes a week and it doesn't feel gross by the end the way some synthetic stuff does. Oh — random tangent, I also got engaged this spring, completely unrelated, ha, but my fiancé actually started coming to my classes after that, which has been fun, he wears Solstice shorts too now actually.

**INTERVIEWER:** Ha, that's great, congratulations. Okay, one more — anything you'd tell another instructor who's on the fence?

**PRIYA:** I'd just say try it for one full teaching day, back to back classes, and see how it feels by the end. For me switching over from CoreForm was honestly night and day, the Pulse is just way more supportive for high-impact stuff, it's not even close in my opinion.
