# Raw interview transcript — Jordan M.

Product discussed: Momentum Legging (compression fit)
Context: Customer interview conducted by Solstice Active marketing team for a case study. Speaker turns labeled below — do not treat unlabeled text as customer voice.

---

**INTERVIEWER:** Thanks for hopping on, Jordan. So, just to start — can you tell me a little about your training and kind of what got you looking for new gear in the first place?

**JORDAN:** Yeah, no, totally, happy to. So I've been running for, gosh, probably eight years now, but I signed up for my first full marathon last year, and training for that is just a totally different animal than the 10Ks I'd been doing. Like, the mileage jump alone. I was running, um, five days a week, and by week six or seven my old leggings were just... they were rolling down at the waist every single run, which, if anyone's run more than like four miles you know how distracting that is, you're constantly tugging at them instead of thinking about your pace.

**INTERVIEWER:** Right, that sounds miserable. So what made you try Solstice specifically?

**JORDAN:** Honestly? My training partner Divya wore the Momentum Legging and I kept noticing hers never moved, like not once, even on our long Sunday runs. So I finally asked her about it and she said the waistband actually has some kind of compression fit thing going on, and I was like okay, worth a shot at this point, I'll try literally anything.

**INTERVIEWER:** And when you got them, what was that first run like?

**JORDAN:** So my first run in them was actually a rest-week easy five miler, nothing crazy, but I remember finishing and being like, wait, I didn't touch the waistband once. Which — okay this sounds small but when you're used to fighting your gear every run, not having to think about it at all is honestly huge. And then I wore them for a 16-miler a few weeks later, which was my longest training run at that point, and same thing, zero rolling, even like two hours in when I was pretty gassed and not exactly running with perfect form anymore.

**INTERVIEWER:** That's great. Did you notice anything else about them, fit-wise or otherwise?

**JORDAN:** Yeah, actually the fabric itself, it's got some stretch to it that I didn't expect — I do a lot of hip-opener stretches before I run, like lunges and stuff, and I was worried they'd feel restrictive but they really move with you. Oh, and — this is maybe a random aside, I also did a half marathon down in Charleston this spring, totally unrelated trip, my sister lives down there, gorgeous course along the water — anyway I wore the leggings for that one too since they'd become kind of my go-to at that point.

**INTERVIEWER:** Nice, sounds like a good trip. So, thinking about the actual marathon day — how did the leggings hold up over the full 26.2?

**JORDAN:** Honestly they were great the whole way, I didn't have to adjust them once in like four and a half hours, which if you'd told me that a year ago I wouldn't have believed you. I will say — and I don't mean this as like a knock on anyone — I'd tried the Vertex Pro leggings before switching, and these are honestly just way better than Vertex, no comparison, the waistband alone makes it not close for me.

**INTERVIEWER:** Got it, that's really helpful context. Last question — would you recommend these to another runner training for their first marathon?

**JORDAN:** A hundred percent. I'd tell them the same thing Divya told me, honestly — worth a shot, and for me it ended up being the thing that let me stop thinking about my gear and just focus on the miles.
